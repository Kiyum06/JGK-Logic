## Project Overview

JGK Logic is a professional ecommerce web application focused on selling gymnastics quipment and training gear.

The platform is designed to simulate a real online store where the users can browse, view and eventually purchase high-quality gymnastics products used for training and fitness development.

## Product Category
Gymnastics Equipment & Training Gear

# Products
- Balance beams
- Parallel bars
- Training mats
- Resistance Bands
- Stretching equipment
- Hand grips 
- Valuting tables
- Rings
